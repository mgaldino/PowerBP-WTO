## Findings

1. **[CRITICAL] Interior separating nonexistence is false.**

   The derivation correctly rules out separation conditional on all weak nonproposers voting yes, but incorrectly extends that conclusion to histories with weak rejection. See derivation Section 5, interface positive-no-delay cell, and ledger claim `N4-CLM-005`.

   Counterexample: `m=3`, `beta=0.9`, `o_0=0.2`, `o_1=0.5`, `nu=0.5`, so `nu_2=0.375`. Let both weak nonproposers vote no; let `H_0` vote no and `H_1` vote yes. The ballot fails, and Bayes gives posterior 0 after `H_0`'s vote and 1 after `H_1`'s vote.

   The weak continuation values are `d_0=0.24` and `z=0.15`, so the proposer's expected continuation is `S=0.195`. `H_0` strictly prefers no (`0.18`) to deviating to yes (`0.45`); `H_1` is indifferent and `T^Y` selects yes. Each weak no is sequentially rational and stage-undominated: a unilateral yes can be assigned posterior 1, yielding `z=0.15`, while no yields `0.195` on path.

   Off path, two weak no votes and posterior-contingent beliefs can give the proposer continuation `0.165<S`, so proposer deviations are deterred. This is a valid pure-ballot PBE with weak stage-undominance. It does not impose weak-vote-passive: weak votes are type-independent, while `H`'s vote generates the Bayes update.

2. **[MAJOR] “Delay iff `A_D(nu)`” and the claimed coverage correspondence are false.**

   In the counterexample above, `nu=0.5>=nu_2`, so `A_D(nu)` is false, yet R1 fails with probability one and R2 is reached. Thus the positive-prior no-delay cell is not pooling-only. The four domain cells partition the primitive domain, but the listed equilibrium records are not exhaustive.

3. **[MAJOR] The `nu=0` endpoint correspondence is incomplete for `m>=3`.**

   At `nu=0`, with at least two weak nonproposers voting no, `H_0` can vote no and `H_1` can vote yes. The low type's no induces posterior 0 and is strictly optimal; the zero-prior high type is indifferent and selects yes under `T^Y`. Two weak no votes can again sustain the failure path and punish deviations. This branch is absent from the endpoint records.
