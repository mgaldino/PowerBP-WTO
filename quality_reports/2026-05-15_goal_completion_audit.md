## Scope exclusions

The following are documented future extensions, not blockers for the current
goal:

1. Unrestricted all-PBE characterization. The Rmd deliberately states selected
   values under the maintained weak-vote-passive pure-threshold assessment and
   does not claim uniqueness over all PBEs.
2. Exact recursive `pi_H>0` game. The current goal treats `pi_H>0` as an
   extension/robustness exercise; the Rmd provides selection-free
   initial-recognition bounds and marks the full recursive game as pending.
3. Empirical OPEC calibration. The Rmd now forbids calibration language unless
   future empirical inputs are supplied.
4. Transport to `formal_model_v5.Rmd`. This was explicitly prohibited during
   the redesign proof pass and remains a later manuscript-integration step.
