# R2 unanimity conditional interface
Source: architecture_note.Rmd section 2.3 equations (3)-(4); historical N2 read with August21 support errata and September deletion of cap.
At every feasible proposal weak respondents Y; H Y iff x_H>=o. Passing N impossible by quota.
pstar=(h-ell)/(1-ell). At entry mu<=pstar propose ell, others zero; above pstar propose h.
Before recognition, weak C=(1-mu)(1-ell)/m for mu<=pstar, else (1-h)/m.
H vector=(ell,h) below and at cutoff, else (h,h). All values native R2, no beta.
At proposer equality low offer selected by minimum H expected payoff. Support from initial prior, Bayes where possible, same freedom per ballot otherwise.
Known public type o: propose o; weak C=(1-o)/m; H C=o.
Closed as conditional derivation only.
